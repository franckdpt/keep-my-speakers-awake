Check for updates at app startup, including login when **Open at login** is enabled,
then every 24 hours while KMSA stays open. Sleep and session returns catch up on a
due check; connection failures retry after one hour.

A discreet dot on the menu-bar speaker indicates an available update. The panel
offers **Install Update… / Installer la mise à jour…** and explicitly confirms
when KMSA is up to date. Background checks never open dialogs or install updates.
