Use a compact native update button above the version footer. It switches from
**Check for Updates / Chercher une mise à jour** to **Update to x.x.x / Mettre à
jour vers x.x.x** when a release is available. Clicking Update starts the download
directly and replaces the button with real transfer progress, followed by
verification/restart progress. Once complete, the slot shows **KMSA is up to date /
KMSA est à jour**, including after the app relaunches.
