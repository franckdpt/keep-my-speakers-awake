Smart Mode now keeps the speaker wake-up schedule active while another app plays
audio on the Mac's compatible jack, including with the screen asleep or locked.

- Removed the 50% Mac-volume cutoff and the two-minute output-audio grace period.
- Kept the ten-minute schedule during screen sleep and locking.
- Added audio-activity notifications and App Nap timing protection during listening;
  the display and the Mac can still sleep normally.
- Preserved microphone, unknown-activity, jack, user-session and system-sleep protections.

Le mode intelligent maintient désormais les réveils pendant une lecture audio,
quel que soit le volume du Mac, même écran éteint ou verrouillé. Les protections
du micro, de la sortie jack, de la session utilisateur et de la veille réelle du
Mac restent actives.
